WHY CURIOSTAR?

Astrology doesn't have to feel complicated.

Astrology is not scientifically established as a method for predicting personality or future events. Use the experience for entertainment, curiosity and personal reflection, and don't use it as a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice.
